Welcome to Felukka! 🚀

This is our first post, and we're excited to kick things off with a simple "Hello World." It's a small step, but it marks the beginning of something we believe in deeply — building tools that bring calm to chaos in the world of cloud-native infrastructure.

## Why Felukka?

We started Felukka because we were tired of seeing operators drown in dashboard walls, YAML sprawl, and cognitive overload. We believe that Kubernetes operations should feel as intuitive as reading a nautical chart — clear, calm, and purposeful.

## What's Next?

Over the coming weeks, we'll be sharing:

- Deep dives into our open-source projects
- Operator playbooks and best practices
- Architectural decisions and trade-offs
- Community contributions and updates

## Get Involved

We're always looking for passionate engineers, designers, and writers who share our vision. Check out our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/felukka) and join the [Discord Bridge](https://discord.gg/Rzt9NM7RK5) to connect with the community.
